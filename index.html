Questions

Frequently Asked

What is your typical turnaround time?

Standard digital printing takes 2-3 business days. Offset printing and custom packaging typically take 5-7 business days depending on the complexity of the finishes.

Do you offer design services?

Yes, our in-house design team can help create your artwork from scratch or refine your existing files to ensure they are print-ready.

What file formats do you accept?

We prefer high-resolution PDF files with crop marks and bleed, but we also accept AI, PSD, and high-res JPG files.
